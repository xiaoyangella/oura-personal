Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

My Oura Data is a private application used only by its owner to access the owner's Oura account.

Data accessed

The application accesses only Oura data covered by the permissions granted during Oura authorization, such as daily sleep, readiness, and activity data.

Purpose and storage

The data is used only to display it to the account owner. OAuth credentials are stored on the owner's device for account access. Retrieved Oura data is processed locally and is not retained after the viewing session.

Sharing

The application does not sell or share Oura data with other people, third-party services, or AI platforms.

Deletion and access

The owner can stop using the application and delete its locally stored credentials at any time. Access to the Oura account can also be revoked through Oura's authorization controls.
